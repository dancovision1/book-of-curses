Book of Curses

Virtual credits only. 5 reels × 3 rows · 10 fixed paylines. The Book is Wild and Scatter.

Base game

  • Wins pay left to right on a payline. The Book substitutes for any paying symbol.
  • Paytable values are × line bet. Line bet = total bet ÷ 10.
  • 3 or more Books anywhere on the grid start Free Spins. 4 or 5 Books still start the same bonus (they do not add extra spins on the trigger).
  • Nothing from a previous bonus carries into the base game. Curse Energy, Curse level, Cursed Symbols and infection all reset when the Book closes.

Free Spins — how a bonus starts

  • You receive 10 Free Spins. The stake of the triggering spin is used for the whole bonus.
  • The Book opens and randomly chooses one paying symbol (not the Book). That is the Cursed Symbol for this bonus only.
  • A new bonus always starts at CURSE I — Awakened, energy 0. There is no “saved curse” between sessions.

Expanding Cursed Symbol

  • If the Cursed Symbol appears on 3 or more different reels, those reels fill top-to-bottom with that symbol.
  • The expand pays the 3 / 4 / 5-of-a-kind value on all 10 paylines (classic Book math), then applies the current Curse multiplier.
  • Ordinary line wins still pay, except lines of the same symbol that just expanded — those are not paid twice.
  • One Cursed Symbol on a single reel does nothing special. Two reels do nothing. The third reel is the unlock.

Curse Meter — during this bonus only

Energy is the only progress track. Levels never go down inside a bonus.

  • Successful expand on 3 reels: +1 energy
  • 4 reels: +2
  • 5 reels: +3
  • Retrigger (3 Books during Free Spins): +5 spins and +1 energy
  • Infection cells that later expand: +1 extra energy
  • 0–2 energy · CURSE I Awakened — expand pays ×1
  • 3–5 · CURSE II Corrupted — expand pays ×2
  • 6–8 · CURSE III Possessed — the Book picks a second Cursed Symbol. Both can expand on the same spin. Multiplier stays ×2
  • 9+ · FULL CURSE — infection unlocks. Expand multiplier ×2 (×3 is reserved if math is retuned later)

What persists — and what does not

Inside one Free Spins session (including retriggers):

  • Kept: remaining spins, Curse Energy, Curse level, both Cursed Symbols, “Full Curse already unlocked”, running bonus win.
  • Kept for the next Free Spin only: a few infected cells may stay on the grid (~18% of them). That is the only carry from spin to spin besides the meter.
  • Retriggers do not reset the meter and do not pick a new first symbol. They only add spins and one energy.

When the Book closes (0 spins left, or you return to the base game):

  • Wiped: energy, Curse level, Cursed Symbols, second symbol, infection leftovers, Full Curse flag.
  • The next time you land 3 Books you start again at CURSE I with a newly chosen symbol. You cannot “keep” a Possessed or Full Curse bonus for later.
  • Your credit balance stays. That is the only thing that survives across bonuses.

FULL CURSE — infection

  • The first time energy reaches 9 in this bonus, the Full Curse ceremony plays.
  • On that spin and every later Free Spin of the same bonus, Cursed Symbols can infect neighbouring cells (up / down / left / right), chance 45% per neighbour, one wave.
  • After infection the game re-checks expands. A bigger expand replaces a smaller one for that symbol; it does not pay both.
  • Infection does not continue after the bonus ends.

Max win

Any single evaluation is capped at 10,000 × total bet. If the cap hits, the Book closes and the “YOU SHOULDN’T HAVE OPENED IT.” presentation plays.
